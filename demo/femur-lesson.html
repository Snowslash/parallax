Explore a separate whole-femur model
What should I look for?

Treat the wire tip as the planned screw tip. Measure from the tip to the gold dot in each image. For this exercise, check both:

  • The two distances add up to 25 mm or less.
  • The tip is within 8 mm of the head centre on the lateral view.

Background reading: Baumgaertner et al. (1995) on tip–apex distance and screw cut-out (PubMed, new tab).
